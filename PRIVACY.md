# Roof Access privacy policy

*Effective 11 November 2026.*

Roof Access is an Android home screen app made by Sarcastic Worm. This policy explains what the app accesses, what leaves your phone, and why. In short, there is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer never receives your data.

## What stays on your phone

These are used only on your device. They are never sent to the developer or anyone else.

- **Your apps and home screen layout:** icons, folders, widgets and settings.
- **Notifications (optional):** with notification access, Roof Access counts unread notifications to show dots or numbers on icons, and lets search find recent WhatsApp messages. Notifications are read on the phone and not stored.
- **Contacts (optional):** with the contacts permission, search can find your contacts.
- **Calendar (optional):** with the calendar permission, your next event can appear under the clock.
- **Accessibility service (optional):** used only to lock the screen and open recent apps when you use those gestures. It doesn't read the screen or what you type.
- **Fingerprint or screen lock (optional):** used to confirm sensitive actions, such as unlocking a door, and to show hidden apps. Android handles this; Roof Access never sees your fingerprint.
- **Photos you pick:** a photo you choose as your wallpaper is set as the wallpaper and not kept or sent anywhere.

## What leaves your phone, and to whom

| Data | Sent to | When | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Your location, **rounded to about 1 km** | [MET Norway](https://api.met.no) and, if that fails, [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com) | Only if you allow location and the weather comes from "This phone"; at most about every 30 minutes while the home screen is open | To show the weather for your area. Neither service gets your exact location or anything that identifies you. |
| What you type in search | Google | Only with "Web suggestions while typing" on, or when you choose to search the web | To show Google's search suggestions and results. Covered by Google's privacy policy. |
| A question you type in search | The Claude or Gemini app on your phone, or claude.ai in your browser if the app isn't installed | Only when you tap "Ask Claude" or "Ask Gemini" | To answer your question. Covered by Anthropic's or Google's privacy policy. |
| Requests to your Home Assistant server, with the token you created | **Your own** Home Assistant server | Only with Home Assistant support on | To show and control your devices. The server is yours; the developer never sees it. |

Weather data is provided by MET Norway and Open-Meteo under the CC BY 4.0 licence.

The optional Google Discover page is shown by the Google app on your phone, under Google's own privacy policy.

## Stored data and backups

- **On the phone:** settings, layout, the last weather reading (with the rounded location it was for) and your Home Assistant address and token. They're stored privately in the app; the token is kept separately from other settings.
- **Android backup:** your phone's own Google backup includes your layout and settings so they return on a new phone. Your Home Assistant token isn't included.
- **Backup files:** "Save to file" and "Save to Google Drive" create a file that you choose where to keep. Automatic backups are saved in a folder you pick on the phone. The token is never written to backup files.

## Your choices

Every permission is optional and can be turned off in Android's settings or in Roof Access's Settings. Turning off weather from the phone, contacts, calendar or web suggestions stops that data being used. Uninstalling Roof Access deletes everything it stored on the phone.

## Children

Roof Access isn't directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.
