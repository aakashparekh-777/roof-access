# What's new

## 0.1.41: first test release (November 2026)

The first version for testers.

- **Home screen:** a paged icon grid with a dock, folders, widgets and your choice of gestures.
- **Search:** apps, contacts and the web from one box, with optional Ask Claude / Ask Gemini.
- **Look:** seasonal live wallpaper with real weather, icon shapes and packs, notification badges, and a choice of animations with a live preview.
- **Home Assistant (optional):** device tiles, alerts under the clock, fingerprint confirmation for locks, garage and alarm, and your own house on the wallpaper, with windows, garage doors, car and visitors that follow your devices.
- **Moving in:** import your Nova Launcher layout, and back up to a file or Google Drive.
